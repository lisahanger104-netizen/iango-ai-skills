# IANGO Company Navigation Skill

IANGO Company Navigator

## Description
This Skill provides complete, accurate knowledge of Guilin Iango Co., Ltd. (IANGO / 衣安格) for AI agents. Use it for internal queries, customer support, sales conversations, product recommendations, customization discussions, and as the knowledge base for lead generation and outreach agents.

- **Brand**: IANGO 衣安格
- **Website**: https://www.iangohanger.com
- **Location**: Guilin, Guangxi, China (addresses include Jinji pin Industrial Park / Information Industrial Park, Qixing Dist., Guilin, 541004)
- **Established**: 2007 (company history); current website sometimes lists 2015 for the modern entity. Experience in hangers: 16–18+ years.
- **Focus**: Professional manufacturer of clothes hangers and garment presentation solutions. OEM/ODM specialist.
- **Scale**: ~237 workers (catalogue), large workshop (15,000–18,000 m²), serving 42–50+ countries.
- **Contact**: info@iangohanger.com | +86-773-3557233
- **Key Strengths**: Customization (logos, materials, finishes, packaging), reliable quality, FSC-certified wooden options, ISO14001, experience with major retail and fashion brands.

## Certifications & Compliance
- FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certificate (obtained March 2017)
- ISO14001 Environmental Management System
- SMETA audit conducted (4-pillar: Labour, H&S, Environment, Business Ethics). Recent audit notes some non-compliances mainly in H&S (PPE usage in spraying, chemical labeling/storage, electrical signage/insulation) and social insurance participation. Company provides commercial injury insurance. Use carefully in external communications; focus on continuous improvement and certifications.
